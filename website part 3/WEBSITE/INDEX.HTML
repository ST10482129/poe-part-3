WELCOME TO KHULA

Baking for our community, one loaf at a time.

Khula Community Bakery is a fictional small bakery
based in Benoni, Gauteng. Established in 2023,
the bakery was created to provide affordable,
fresh baked products while creating part-time
employment opportunities for young people.

From everyday bread to celebration cakes and
catering packages, we aim to provide quality
products and friendly service to our community.
